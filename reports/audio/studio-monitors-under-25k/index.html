Buying Guide — Where the ₹25k Goes

Budget allocation

Monitors ≤ ₹20k + audio interface ~₹8.5k (Focusrite Scarlett Solo) + isolation pads ~₹1.5-2.5k. An interface's balanced outputs kill the hum/hiss of laptop headphone jacks.

Room acoustics beat speakers

Expensive monitors in an untreated bedroom sound worse than cheap monitors in a treated room. Spend on panels/pads at first reflection points before maxing the speaker budget.

Placement is free

Tweeters at ear level, speakers forming an equilateral triangle with your head, 6-12" from the wall. Decouple from the desk (pads or folded towel) to stop it resonating like a subwoofer.

Bluetooth = reference only

BT adds 30-40ms latency — unusable for real-time mixing. Wired TRS/XLR for work, Bluetooth only for casual listening. Mix with a sub-$15k monitor's limits in mind; cross-check low-end on headphones.
